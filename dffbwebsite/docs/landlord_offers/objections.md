Landlord Objection Handling
---------------------------

Common objections and short responses:

- "I’m worried about risk/tenant issues."
  - Response: We take on operational risk under Master Lease; insurance, tenant screening, and a dedicated ops team protect the property.

- "I want control over my property."
  - Response: We offer structures (Master Lease with clear maintenance clauses or JV with governance) that preserve owner protections.

- "Legal and compliance concerns." 
  - Response: We provide a transparent agreement outline for counsel review and carry required insurance and licensing.

- "What if you fail?" 
  - Response: Contracts include performance covenants, notice periods, and buy-back/breach remedies to protect the owner.
