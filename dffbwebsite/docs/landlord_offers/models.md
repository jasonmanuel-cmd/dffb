Landlord Offer Models
=====================

Three clear offer models for landlords:

- Master Lease: DFFB leases the property from the landlord at a fixed monthly rate and operates the senior housing business. Landlord receives stable, contracted income and minimal operational involvement.

- Net Profit Partnership: Landlord retains ownership and receives a baseline rent plus a share of net operating profit. Landlord benefits from upside while DFFB manages operations and risk-sharing.

- Joint Venture: Landlord and DFFB form a joint entity to convert and operate the property. Both parties share equity, upside, and governance according to agreed percentages.
