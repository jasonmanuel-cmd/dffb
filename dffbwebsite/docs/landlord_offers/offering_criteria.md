Model Offering Criteria
-----------------------

Quick rules to choose which model to propose:

- Master Lease: property in good condition, owner wants no ops, prefers stable rent.
- Net Profit Partnership: owner wants some upside, comfortable with financial transparency.
- Joint Venture: owner willing to co-invest or trade equity for higher long-term returns.

Also consider: local zoning, required upgrades, owner liquidity needs, succession plans.
