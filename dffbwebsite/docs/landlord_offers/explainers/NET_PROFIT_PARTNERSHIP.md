Net Profit Partnership — One‑Page Explainer
------------------------------------------

What it is:

DFFB operates the property; landlord keeps ownership and receives a steady base payment plus a percentage of net profits.

What you get:
- Base rent plus upside when the business performs
- Ongoing involvement optional — you remain owner
- Revenue share aligns incentives

Why landlords like it:
- Participation in potential upside without day-to-day operations
- Strong alignment with operator performance

Basic safeguards:
- Clear definitions of "net profit," audit rights, minimum guarantees, and termination triggers

Ideal for:
- Owners open to shared financial upside and modest operational tolerance
