Joint Venture — One‑Page Explainer
---------------------------------

What it is:

DFFB and the landlord create a joint entity to convert and operate the property as senior housing. Both parties share equity, profits, and governance.

What you get:
- Equity upside as the property value and business scale
- Shared decision-making and aligned long-term incentives
- Professional operations run by DFFB

Why landlords like it:
- Potentially higher returns than passive leasing
- Continued participation in the asset’s long-term value

Basic protections:
- Governance rules, capital contribution terms, buy/sell mechanics, and clear exit options

Ideal for:
- Owners willing to share control for higher long-term upside
