Master Lease — One‑Page Explainer
---------------------------------

What it is:

You lease the property to DFFB under a fixed-term lease. We operate the senior housing business and pay you a reliable monthly rent.

What you get:
- Predictable, contracted income (no variable ops risk)
- No daily management or staffing headaches
- Property preserved and professionally maintained

Why landlords like it:
- Income stability similar to a corporate tenant
- Low involvement — you remain an asset owner
- Easy to budget and report to lenders or family

Basic landlord protections:
- Lease term, maintenance obligations, insurance, and clear exit/renewal clauses

Ideal for:
- Owners who want steady cash flow and minimal risk
