Agreement Templates — Outline for Legal Drafting
-----------------------------------------------

Files to prepare for counsel:

- Master Lease: term, rent schedule, maintenance responsibilities, insurance, default remedies, subordination, inspection rights, renewals.
- Net Profit Partnership: base payment, definition of "net profit," accounting/audit rights, minimum guarantee, distribution waterfall, termination triggers.
- Joint Venture: entity formation, capital contributions, ownership percentages, governance, distributions, buy-sell, valuation method, exit mechanics.

Recommend: have landlord counsel and DFFB counsel prepare final templates; these outlines are negotiation-ready.
