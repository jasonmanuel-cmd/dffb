RCFE Distress Signs — Quick Checklist
-----------------------------------

Use these signals when screening operators or facilities. Any 2+ of these merits a deeper outreach.

- Low occupancy (below market average for the area)
- Frequent staff turnover or visible understaffing
- Repeated negative online reviews mentioning care or safety
- Deferred maintenance or visible property deterioration
- Licensing warnings, fines, or compliance notices
- Financial signs: late property taxes, utility shutoffs, eviction filings
- Owner/operator advanced age with no clear succession plan
- Poor marketing / out-of-date online presence
- Unusually low or volatile pricing compared to peers
- High resident turnover or short average stay

Scoring suggestion: mark each sign present = 1, total >=2 = follow-up priority.
