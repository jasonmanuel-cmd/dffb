Soft-Entry Outreach — Operators
-------------------------------

Email (soft, empathetic):

Subject: A quick, no-pressure question about [Facility Name]

Hi [Name],

I’m [Your Name] with DFFB. We help long-running RCFE operators reduce day-to-day stress and improve occupancy while protecting staff and resident continuity. No sales pitch — I’d simply like to understand if your facility has any capacity or staffing pressures we could help with now or plan for later.

Would you be open to a 10–15 minute call this week? If so, which time works best?

Thank you for running an important service in our community — I’ll be respectful of your time.

Regards,
[Your Name] — DFFB


Voicemail (20–30s):
"Hi [Name], this is [Your] from DFFB. We work with small RCFE operators to stabilize occupancy, reduce staff churn, and keep your legacy intact. I’m not selling anything right now — just want to ask two quick questions about capacity and staffing. Please call me at [number] or email [email]. Thank you."


SMS (very short):
"Hi [Name], [Your] from DFFB — quick question about your facility capacity. Free 10-min call? Reply yes/no"


LinkedIn / In-person opener (brief):
"I work with operators to reduce admissions gaps and strengthen staffing — happy to share a short playbook if you’re open."

Follow-up (if no reply after 5–7 days): send a brief note offering a free, no-obligation facility assessment or a one-page checklist on occupancy improvement.
