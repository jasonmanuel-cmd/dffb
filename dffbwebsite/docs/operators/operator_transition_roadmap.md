Operator Transition Roadmap (0–36 months)
---------------------------------------

Phase 0 — Intro & Assessment (0–30 days)
- Quick 10–15 minute call, request basic facility data (beds, occupancy, staffing).
- Perform a rapid risk/ops assessment and share a 1‑page stabilization plan.

Phase 1 — Stabilize (0–3 months)
- Address critical compliance and safety issues.
- Provide short-term staffing support or referral options.
- Implement quick marketing and intake fixes to lift occupancy.

Phase 2 — Optimize (3–9 months)
- Implement systems: intake funnel, referral network, simple P&L tracking.
- Train retained staff and implement retention incentives.
- Monthly performance review and transparent reporting.

Phase 3 — Transition Planning (6–18 months)
- Co-design a succession path if desired: gradual handover, revenue-share, or JV terms.
- Agree milestones for governance, staffing, and financial thresholds.

Phase 4 — Handover / Long-term Operation (12–36 months)
- Execute the agreed succession or long-term partnership terms.
- Ensure staff contracts, resident communication, and legal documents are finalized.
- Capture proof assets (metrics, testimonials) for future investor and landlord conversations.

Governance & Communication
- Weekly standups during Stabilize phase, biweekly during Optimize, monthly during Transition.
- Preserve staff roles where possible; offer retention bonuses tied to performance milestones.

Exit & Protection Mechanics
- Define clear milestones and exit triggers in an MOU before deep work begins.
- Maintain resident continuity plans and communicate transparently to families.
