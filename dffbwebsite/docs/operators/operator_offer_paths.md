Operator Offer Paths — Three Options
-----------------------------------

1) Operational Partnership
- What: DFFB provides operational oversight while current operator remains involved in day-to-day or advisory role.
- Benefits to operator: immediate relief with retained role, improved marketing and intake, shared operational expertise.
- Typical terms: short-term management fee + performance bonuses; 6–12 month stabilization period.
- Timeline: stabilize (0–3 months), optimize (3–9 months), reassess long-term structure (9–12 months).

2) Gradual Exit (Phased Succession)
- What: Operator transitions responsibilities to DFFB over 12–36 months with staged milestones and staff retention guarantees.
- Benefits: predictable handover, income continuity, preservation of legacy and staff jobs.
- Typical terms: revenue share during transition, fixed exit price or buy-in option, staff retention commitments.
- Timeline: 12–36 months depending on owner preference.

3) Network Affiliation (Referrals + Support)
- What: Operator joins DFFB’s network for marketing, placement, and compliance support while retaining ownership and control.
- Benefits: higher occupancy through shared intake, access to placement network, lower marketing costs.
- Typical terms: subscription or referral fee structure; optional performance incentives.
- Timeline: onboarding 30–60 days.

For each path prepare: sample timeline, simple P&L showing expected occupancy lift, staff retention plan, and a short MOU for initial engagement.
