Operator Onboarding — Step-by-step Checklist
-------------------------------------------

Goal: fast, repeatable onboarding that minimizes disruption and establishes measurable early wins.

Pre-onboard (MOU & Intro)
- Sign non-binding MOU / Letter of Intent outlining scope, pilot length, data access, and confidentiality.
- Collect basic facility data: bed count, current occupancy, staff list, licenses, P&L (if available), resident census.

Day 0–30: Rapid Assessment & Stabilization
- Perform compliance and safety triage; list required immediate fixes.
- Deploy short-term staffing support or placement options if critical.
- Implement quick marketing fixes (update listings, call local referral partners, promote availability).
- Provide simple SOPs for intake and referral handling.

30–90 days: Systems & Early Optimization
- Set up intake funnel: referral contacts, standardized intake form, response SLA.
- Install basic reporting: weekly occupancy, weekly admissions, staffing levels, cashflow snapshot.
- Train existing staff on intake and documentation (one 60–90 minute session + checklist).
- Start monthly performance review meetings; agree KPIs.

90–180 days: Growth & Transition Planning
- Improve resident placement pipeline and referral partnerships.
- Implement retention incentives for key staff; document roles and succession candidates.
- If moving toward succession or JV, define governance, milestones, and financial transition terms.

Ongoing (post-180 days)
- Monthly reporting and quarterly business review.
- Continuous improvement playbook: marketing A/B, pricing strategy, local partnerships.
- Capture testimonials, metrics, and financials for proof assets.

Operational items to prepare
- Required documents: licenses, insurance certificates, staff rosters, resident agreements, vendor contracts.
- Access: key holders, EMR or resident files (view-only), payment portals for billing.
- Communication plan: resident & family notification template, staff FAQ, referral partner announcement.

Tools and templates to provide
- 1-page stabilization plan
- Intake form template
- Staff training checklist
- Basic weekly KPI tracker (spreadsheet)
- Resident/family communication templates

Timeline & ownership
- DFFB lead: operations manager (owns stabilization & intake setup)
- Operator lead: facility manager (owns staff, on-the-ground execution)
- Weekly cadence: stabilization; Monthly cadence: performance & roadmap

Exit Criteria for Pilot
- Agreed KPI improvement (e.g., occupancy +15 points or X new admissions) or mutual decision to end pilot.
