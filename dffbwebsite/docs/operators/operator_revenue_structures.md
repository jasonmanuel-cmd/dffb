Operator Revenue-Share Structures
--------------------------------

Purpose: clear, negotiable templates to propose to RCFE operators. Include safeguards and simple examples.

1) Management Fee + Performance Bonus
- Structure: fixed monthly management fee (covers baseline ops) + quarterly performance bonus based on occupancy or net revenue targets.
- Example: $2,500/month + 10% of net revenue above 85% occupancy.
- Safeguards: defined KPI calculations, minimum service level agreement (SLA), 30-day termination notice.

2) Net Profit Share (Simple)
- Structure: Operator retains ownership; DFFB manages operations and receives X% of audited net profit after agreed adjustments.
- Example: 70/30 split (Operator 70%, DFFB 30%) after agreed operating expenses; minimum guarantee of $1,500/month to operator for first 6 months.
- Safeguards: clear "net profit" definition, monthly statements, audit rights, holdback for disputed items.

3) Tiered Revenue Share
- Structure: revenue share increases as occupancy or revenue thresholds are hit to reward scaling performance.
- Example: 0–75% occupancy = DFFB 15% of net; 76–90% = 25%; 91%+ = 35%.
- Safeguards: baseline occupancy measurement window, reconciliations.

4) Base Rent + Upside Pool (Hybrid)
- Structure: landlord/operator receives base rent; DFFB receives an upside pool (percentage of surplus revenue) distributed after reserve and capex.
- Example: base rent $2,000/month; upside pool = 30% of net operating income over $30,000 annual threshold.
- Safeguards: waterfall, reserve for capex, timing of distributions.

5) Phased Succession Revenue Share
- Structure: during transition period revenue is shared with a shifting split moving toward DFFB or the new manager over 12–36 months.
- Example: Year 1: Operator 80% / DFFB 20%; Year 2: 60/40; Year 3: 50/50 or final agreed split.
- Safeguards: milestones, staff retention clauses, buyout formula.

Common clauses to include in all structures
- Minimum guarantees or base payments for the landlord/operator during initial months
- Clear accounting definitions and monthly statements
- Audit and inspection rights for financials and resident files (limited and privacy-compliant)
- Termination events and cure periods
- Resident continuity and staff retention protections
- Insurance and indemnity allocation

Negotiation tips
- Start with a minimum guarantee to reduce owner anxiety.
- Use simple, auditable KPIs (occupancy, verified revenue) rather than complex line-item accounting early on.
- Offer short pilot periods (3–6 months) with clear exit terms to lower friction.
