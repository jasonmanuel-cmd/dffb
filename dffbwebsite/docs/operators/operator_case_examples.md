Operator Case Examples — Before / After
-------------------------------------

Case 1 — Small home (6 beds)
- Before: 55% occupancy, frequent short-staffing, owner age 72 planning retirement, monthly rent income low and inconsistent.
- Intervention: DFFB provided marketing + placement support, temporary management help for 3 months, staff retention incentives.
- After (6 months): occupancy 85%, additional net revenue $3,000/month, owner retained part-time advisory role, full staff retained.

Case 2 — Multi-bed house (10 beds)
- Before: 65% occupancy, poor online presence, 2 compliance warnings, owner overwhelmed.
- Intervention: compliance triage, minor property repairs, centralized intake, and placement pipeline.
- After (9 months): occupancy 90%, compliance issues resolved, revenue up 30%, operator reports lower stress and regained time.

Case 3 — Succession (owner 78, no successor)
- Before: owner working 60+ hours/week, no clear succession plan, occupancy 70%.
- Intervention: phased succession plan, revenue-share during transition, staff retention plan, community outreach to boost referrals.
- After (18 months): owner reduced hours to advisory role, revenue stable, new local manager hired from retained staff, owner legacy preserved.

Each case should be adapted with real numbers once pilot data is available; these examples are templates for pitch discussions.
