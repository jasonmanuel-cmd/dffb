Succession Partner — Positioning Statement
----------------------------------------

Headline: "Help keep the residents safe and the legacy intact — not a takeover."

Core message:

We partner with long-running RCFE operators who want continuity for residents and relief for owners. Our approach is supportive and phased: we help stabilize operations, modernize marketing and intake, and create a voluntary succession plan that preserves the operator’s legacy and staff where possible.

Key points to emphasize with operators:
- This is about continuity, not replacement.
- We prioritize staff retention and resident well‑being.
- Transitions are gradual and co-designed; owner/manager control can be retained during the handover.
- We provide operational support, compliance assistance, and access to a network of placements and referrals.

Call-to-action: "If you’re thinking about who will run this place in the future — let’s talk about a low‑risk plan that protects residents and your legacy."
