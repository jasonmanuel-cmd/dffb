# Distressed & Inherited Property Lead Sources

Primary sources to build weekly outreach lists:

## Probate & Estate Sources (highest intent)
- County probate records (public, searchable by county courthouse; fees vary $5–20/access)
- Probate attorney networks (partner with estate/probate lawyers; they refer heirs who need guidance)
- Estate sale companies (auctioneers and liquidators; they contact heirs; partner for referrals)
- Ancestry/genealogy sites (people researching inheritance often look for asset options)

## Vacant Property & Code Enforcement
- County code enforcement records (public; identifies problem properties and owners)
- Tax assessor delinquent property lists (some counties publish; shows likely distress)
- Vacant property registries (some cities require registration; ask local government)
- Property tax foreclosure notices (published in county legal notices; high-intent prospects)

## Financial Distress Indicators
- Mortgage default/foreclosure notices (public record; shows imminent distress)
- Utility shutoff notices (municipal records; weaker signal but available)
- Lien filings (UCC-1, judgment liens published in county records)

## Real Estate Channels
- Real estate agents (partner with local agents; they know off-market/problem properties)
- MLS vacant/problem property listings (agents can filter)
- Wholesalers and fix-and-flip networks (they often find distressed properties; they refer)

## Online & Data Aggregators
- Zillow / Redfin / Trulia distressed property filters (do they have "foreclosure" or "pre-foreclosure" labels?)
- PublicData / LandWatch type sites (sometimes surface problem properties)
- Facebook groups for local real estate investors (often share distressed leads)

## Direct Outreach Channels
- Door-to-door in neighborhoods with visible vacancy/neglect (high-touch but effective)
- Direct mail to property owners identified via tax records (batch process weekly)
- Cold calls to owners from county records (labor-intensive; use for high-priority targets)

## Referral Channels (build over time)
- Probate attorneys, estate planners, CPAs
- Real estate agents and wholesalers
- Property managers and home inspectors
- Senior living advisors and geriatric care managers

## Quick prioritization
1) Probate records + attorney partnerships = highest ROI, clearest intent
2) Code enforcement + tax delinquency = moderate ROI, clear pain
3) Wholesaler networks + real estate agents = moderate ROI, ongoing referral stream
4) Direct outreach (doors + mail) = lower immediate ROI, high volume

## Recommended weekly cadence
- Pull 5–10 new probate records (or attorney referrals)
- Pull 10–15 code enforcement / tax delinquency records
- 2–3 referral conversations with partners
- 50–100 direct mail pieces or door visits (if doing direct outreach)
