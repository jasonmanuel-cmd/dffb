# Heir / Distressed Owner Outreach Messaging

## Email (soft, empathetic)

**Subject: A practical idea about the property at [address]**

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your Name] with DFFB. I noticed your property at [address] has been [vacant/quiet/in the family transition], and I wanted to reach out with a thought.

We help families and property owners convert inherited or underused homes into managed senior housing communities. Depending on your situation, you might:

- **Earn steady monthly income** while we manage everything (no landlord headaches)
- **Keep ownership but share upside** as we fill the property and grow revenue
- **Sell outright** at fair market value and be done in 30–60 days

Many families in your situation prefer one of these options to a foreclosure, a fire sale, or years of vacancy.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call to see if any option fits your goals? No pressure — just a conversation.

[Your phone] / [Your email]

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Direct Mail (postcard or 1-pager)

**Front:**

- Option 1: Lease it to us → steady monthly income

- Option 3: Sell it to us → cash in 30 days

"Let's talk. [Phone] / [Email]"

## SMS (very short)

"Hi [Name], [Your] from DFFB — quick idea about the property at [address]. Could convert it to senior housing income. Call/text?"

---

## Cold Call (30s script)

"Hi [Name], this is [Your] from DFFB — we help property owners convert inherited or vacant homes into managed senior housing. I noticed your property at [address]. Do you have 2 minutes to hear three practical options for it?"

If yes:
- Option 1: We lease and pay you monthly.
- Option 2: You keep it; we operate and share profits.
- Option 3: We buy it — cash offer in 30 days.

"Which, if any, sounds interesting?"

---

## LinkedIn / Social (for younger heirs)

"Just inherited a property you're not sure what to do with? We specialize in turning problem properties into managed senior housing income. Three flexible options — DM if curious."

---

## Follow-up (if no reply)

After 5–7 days, send one follow-up with a simple 1-pager on the three options and a property assessment offer: "Free evaluation of your property's conversion potential — we'll show you realistic income scenarios."

---

## Key messaging rules
- **Lead with relief, not acquisition.**
- **Offer options, not a single path.**
- **Mention the community good** (senior housing shortage) but don't oversell it.
- **Include a clear CTA** (call, text, email) and a low-barrier next step.
- **Personalize where possible** (address, property details) to show you've done homework.
