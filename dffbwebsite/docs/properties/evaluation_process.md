# Quick Property Evaluation Process

A 30-minute assessment to determine if a property is viable for conversion or not.

---

## Pre-Visit (5 min)

**Gather basics:**
- Address and ownership info
- Current condition (owner description): vacant, occupied, code issues?
- Estimated square footage and layout (beds/baths)
- Any known issues: roof, foundation, electrical, plumbing
- Zoning: residential, commercial, mixed-use?
- Owner expectations: timeline, preferred structure (sale, lease, partnership)

---

## On-Site Assessment (15–20 min)

### Walkthrough Checklist

**Exterior (3 min)**
- Overall condition: well-maintained, moderate repairs needed, major issues?
- Roof and gutters: signs of age/damage?
- Siding: paint, siding material, visible rot?
- Driveway/parking: adequate for residents and staff vehicles?
- Accessible entry: can we accommodate walkers, wheelchairs?

**Interior — Layout & Space (5 min)**
- Number of rooms suitable for conversion (bedrooms, common areas)
- Kitchen: commercial-grade necessary? Current condition?
- Bathrooms: grab bars, accessible showers, adequate count?
- Living spaces: can they serve as activity/dining areas?
- Hallways: wheelchair accessible width (36"+)?

**Systems & Safety (5 min)**
- Electrical: updated panel, adequate capacity for monitoring/equipment?
- HVAC: functional? Accessible for maintenance?
- Plumbing: any signs of issues, adequate hot water capacity?
- Fire safety: sprinklers, alarms, exits clear?
- Flooring: trip hazards, slip risks, easy to clean?

**Zoning & Compliance (3–5 min)**
- Residential or commercial zone?
- Any code violations or notices visible?
- Distance to hospital, emergency services?
- Neighborhood character: quiet, walkable, safe?

---

## Scoring

**Viability Score (1–5 scale):**

| Factor | Scoring |
|--------|---------|
| Layout & Space | 5 = perfect (separate beds, common area, kitchen); 1 = unusable |
| Systems | 5 = modern/updated; 1 = failing, needs major work |
| Safety & Accessibility | 5 = already accessible; 1 = major modifications needed |
| Zoning & Compliance | 5 = already zoned for care; 1 = zoning variance required |
| Curb Appeal & Maintenance | 5 = move-in ready; 1 = major repairs needed |

**Total Score Guidance:**
- **20–23 (Green Light):** Viable for conversion; minor to moderate work
- **15–19 (Yellow Light):** Viable but requires significant investment or zoning work
- **<15 (Red Light):** Not viable or requires prohibitive investment; consider passing or selling to investor

---

## Post-Visit Summary (5 min)

**Quick report to owner:**

*Example (Green Light):*
"Great news — your property is well-suited for a 4–5 bed senior home. Layout is practical, systems are solid, and zoning is residential. We'd estimate [X] weeks of light renovation (bathrooms, kitchen updates, accessibility). Expected occupancy timeline: [X] months post-opening."

*Example (Yellow Light):*
"The property has potential, but we'd need to do [specific work: electrical update, zoning variance, etc.]. This adds [cost/timeline]. Let's discuss whether it makes financial sense."

*Example (Red Light):*
"Honestly, the foundation/roof/[major issue] makes this prohibitively expensive for our model. Your best option is likely [sale to investor, hold and rent, etc.]. I don't want to waste your time on a non-viable deal."

---

## Financial Estimate (if green/yellow)

**Quick rough estimate to include in summary:**
- Estimated conversion cost: $[X]–$[Y] per bed
- Timeline to occupancy: [X] months
- Projected revenue at 80% occupancy: $[X]/month
- Projected net to owner (if revenue share): $[X]/month

---

## Next Steps

**If Green Light:**
- "I'd like to do a more detailed architectural assessment and financials. Can we schedule that?"
- "Shall we draft a simple MOU to explore this further?"

**If Yellow/Red Light:**
- "Let's chat about other options (sale, hold, or different use case)."
- "I'll connect you with a [real estate investor / realtor] who specializes in [property type]."

---

## Tools to Bring

- Checklist (printed or mobile)
- Measuring tape (for accessibility compliance)
- Camera or phone for photos
- Quick one-pager with conversion examples
- Simple financial scenario (sell vs. partner vs. hold)

---

## Tone & Communication

- **Honest:** if it's not viable, say so. Protect your credibility.
- **Empowering:** show the owner what's possible and what the constraints are.
- **Action-oriented:** leave with a clear next step or referral.
- **Professional:** thorough but quick; respect their time.

---

## Referral Outcomes

If property is not viable for DFFB:
- Refer to real estate investors, wholesalers, or flippers
- Suggest local property managers if owner wants to rent it out
- Connect with estate sale companies if owner wants to liquidate
- This builds goodwill and positions DFFB as a trusted resource, not just a buyer
