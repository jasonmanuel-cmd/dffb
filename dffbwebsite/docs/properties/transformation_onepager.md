## Design Notes for Graphic Designer

- **Color scheme:** warm, professional, trust-building (blues, greens)
- **Tone:** hopeful, solution-focused, not corporate or cold
- **Imagery:** before/after properties, smiling seniors, caring staff, community moments
- **Typography:** large, readable headlines; clean body text
- **Layout:** equal visual weight to all three options; avoid favoritism

## Alternative: Digital Version (Landing Page)
